What you can do

Watch reels

Collect words

Play games

Quick word games that turn practice into a few minutes of fun.

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded app-release.apk file.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  4. Tap Install, then open glosy.
